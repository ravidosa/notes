# Egypt and Mesopotamia
## Egypt
records limited
ahmose/ahmes: first known mathematician (rhind papyrus)
	math used by priests, scribes, govt officials
	tool of power, organization
info from rhind papyrus (84 problems), moscow math papyrus, berlin paprus, math leather roll (circa 1650)
practical, no general understanding of theory, math used to organize and goveren (commerce, agriculture, architecture)
numeral system (bouchard, tr by champollion from rosetta)
	hieroglyphic vs hieratic/demotic
	decimal
	size of numbers reflect culture
addition/subtraction: carrying with symbols
multiplication: binary powers, add up
	for all natural numbers, there exists binary representation
method of false position (problem 26)
	$x + \frac{x}{4} = 15$
	plug in denominator of fraction for x
	multiply by factor to make it match
only unit fractions except 2/3 (sacred)
	no trivial decompositions
	divide 6 loaves among 10 men (problem 3)
		$\frac{3}{5} = \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{10} = \bar{2} + \bar{10}$
	how to break down $\frac{2}{n}$ into unit fractions
		assume $n$ odd, $> 2$
		$\frac{2}{n} = \frac{1}{\frac{n+1}{2}} + \frac{1}{\frac{n(n+1)}{2}}$
		erdos straus conjecture: $\frac{4}{n}$ with only three unit fractions
	fraction table
