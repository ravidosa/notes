# Combinatorics
basic principle of counting
	$m$ outcomes a first stage, $n$ outcomes at second stage, $mn$ overall
independent events
	$P(A \cap B) = P(A) \cdot P(B)$
permutations: $P(n,k) = \frac{n!}{(n-k)!}$
combinations: $C(n,k) = {n \choose k} = \frac{n!}{(n-k)!k!}$
	divide into $r$ distinguishable subsets: ${n \choose n_1 \ldots n_r} = \frac{n!}{\prod_r n_r!}$
