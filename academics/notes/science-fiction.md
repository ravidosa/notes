# Science Fiction
## The Origins of Science Fiction
called scientific romance
	unrealistic story involving science (fantastic voyages - jules verne)
	"science fiction" invented in 1929 by hugo gernsback
utopian romance
	thomas more's utopia
	expedition to new world
sci fi = fiction about future
	shift in worldview
	world is constantly changing (scientific revolution)
		greeks, etc thought world was fixed (plato's republic)
	possibility of imagining how future looks
	political and social change (revolutions)
	uniformitarianism (charles lyle), evolution (charles darwin)
sci fi is literature of change - brooke landon

	illegitimate genre

	globalization of sci fi
	racial, gender, class diversity
	sociological, philosophical questions
	interrogate golden age, criticize normative culture
do away with genre conventions, postmodernism
	experimental sci fi
	metanarrative

	very experimental writing style
	explicit about sex, sexuality
ursula k le guin, woman + anthropologist
	jewish woman working in sexist, anti-semitic culture
