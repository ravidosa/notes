\problem{}
Since E ($4$) and T ($19$) are the most common letters in the Englis alphabet, they are the most likely to be mapped to W ($22$) and B ($1$). Then, by the affine transformation:
\begin{align*}
    4a + b & = 22 \bmod 26
    19a + b & = 1 \bmod 26
\end{align*}
It follows that:
\begin{align*}
    15a & = 5 \bmod 26 \\
    a & = 15^{-1} \cdot 5 = 7 \cdot 5 = 11 \bmod 26
    4 \cdot 11 + b & = 22 \bmod 26 \\
    b & = 22 - 4 \cdot 11 = 4 \bmod 26
\end{align*}
We se that $a = 11, b = 4$.
